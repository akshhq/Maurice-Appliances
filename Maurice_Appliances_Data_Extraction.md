# Company Profile & Product Specifications: Maurice Appliances

## Section 1: Corporate Overview

### About Us
Maurice Appliances is an emerging Indian home appliance brand dedicated to enriching everyday life through reliable, innovative, and affordable home solutions. Established in 2010 in Kullu, Himachal Pradesh, the company was founded with a vision to manufacture high-quality appliances tailored to the needs of Indian households while upholding global standards of performance, safety, and durability. 

Over the years, Maurice has grown from a regional manufacturer into a trusted brand with a diverse portfolio of home and kitchen appliances. Built on a foundation of engineering excellence and customer-centric values, the company has earned the confidence of dealers, distributors, and consumers through its consistent focus on quality, reliability, and value.

Every Maurice appliance is thoughtfully designed to combine modern technology, elegant aesthetics, and practical functionality, delivering dependable performance and energy efficiency for today's homes. Backed by ethical business practices, a strong manufacturing foundation, and responsive after-sales support, Maurice Appliances continues to expand its presence while remaining committed to creating products that improve everyday living and build lasting customer trust.

Today, Maurice Appliances continues to strengthen its manufacturing capabilities, expand its nationwide distribution network, and develop innovative products that enhance the lifestyle of millions of families.

## Section 2: Historical Evolution & Milestones

* **2012:**
  - Formally entered the market with the registered brand name **maurice®**.
  - Launched the initial product offering: **Heat Pillar**.
  - Introduced standard **Electric Water Heaters** ranging in capacity from 1 Liter to 25 Liters.
  - Rolled out Inductions and small room heaters.
  - Introduced Table, Wall, Pedestal, and All-Purpose fans.
  - Launched energy-efficient elements featuring maximized heat radiation.
* **2014:**
  - Diversified portfolio by launching **Madhani Gas Stoves** and **Electric Chimneys** for Indian kitchens.
  - Introduced the **Carbon Rod Heat Pillar** to the heating range.
  - Upgraded water heater aesthetics and lifespan by introducing the smart **ABS outer body**.
  - Expanded storage capacities to include heavy-duty **35 Liter** and **50 Liter** Water Heaters.
* **2015:**
  - Launched garment care lines featuring 2 Dry Iron models (**Heavy Weight Iron** & **Steam Iron**).
  - Commissioned multi-location manufacturing facilities in **Bawana, Delhi** and **Jia, Kullu (Himachal Pradesh)**.
  - Introduced Gas-based Room Heaters and Mixer Grinders (2 distinct models).
  - Launched Juicer Mixer Grinders (JMG), Hand Blenders, and mini Choppers.
* **2016:**
  - Developed and launched expanded variants and advanced designs for Room Heaters and Ceiling Fans.
* **2017:**
  - Formally achieved **BIS (ISI) Certification** across core manufacturing lines to guarantee standardized quality metrics.
  - Upgraded manufacturing procedures and optimized advanced laboratory testing hubs.
* **2018:**
  - Initiated highly scaled-up domestic production for state-of-the-art Induction Cooktops.
  - Secured an institutional supply contract from **HPBOCW** under official rate contract criteria as an approved L1 Vendor.
  - Supplied over **45,000 units** of Maurice MIC-21 Induction Cooktops to all separate Sericulture Offices distributed across Himachal Pradesh.
  - Maintained an active rate contract with the **Controller of Stores, Udhyog Bhawan, Shimla**.
  - Manufactured and supplied **95,000 units** of the Maurice Rado Heat Pillar (backed by a 1-year product warranty) directly to a multitude of Government offices within Himachal Pradesh.
* **2019:**
  - Received an incremental order for **9,600 units** of Maurice Rado Heat Pillars from the Himachal Pradesh Government.
  - Introduced the specialized **ABS Body Heat Blowers** series (spanning 5 distinct models across an economical price tier).
  - Launched **Single Rod Heat Pillars** meticulously configured for small commercial cubicles and compact domestic residential spaces.
* **2021:**
  - Entered the small beverage appliance market by launching high-capacity **Electric Kettles** (available in 1.5 Liter and 1.8 Liter variants).
* **2022:**
  - Expanded kitchen essentials to include **Sandwich Toasters**.
  - Introduced automated **Atta Chakki** units calibrated for premium domestic operations.
  - Scaled up air management lines by introducing dedicated Bathroom and Kitchen **Exhaust Fans** alongside heavy-duty **Ventilating Fans**.
